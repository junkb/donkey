= attribution

== lineage

donkey exists because of https://adium.im/[adium], and is built out of adium's
work. source files are imported from adium and adapted, and the architecture
donkey is organized around — the separation of accounts, services, contacts,
chats, and messages — is adium's design, arrived at over more than a decade by
its contributors.

adium is copyright 2001–2013 by the people listed in
link:contributors.adoc[contributors.adoc]. donkey's own code is copyright 2026
the donkey authors, listed there as well.

== affiliation

donkey is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by the adium project,
and does not use adium's name or artwork as its identity.
